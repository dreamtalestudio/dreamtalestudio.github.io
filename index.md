# DreamTale — قصص الأحلام

**AI-powered personalized bedtime stories for children.**

**قصة ما قبل النوم مخصصة باسم طفلك — مصوّرة ومسموعة، جاهزة خلال ثوانٍ.**

DreamTale creates magical, illustrated bedtime stories starring your child — generated on demand with words, pictures, and narration. No ads. No accounts. Your stories stay on your device.
